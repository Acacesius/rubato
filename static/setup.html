Step 1 of 5

You, the host

Your name shows up as “name (host)” whenever you touch the queue, volume or playback. The host key opens the dashboard: the volume cap, the speaker key, relinking YouTube. Keep it private.

Step 2 of 5

The speaker

One device plays the music: a laptop on the stereo, an old phone on a Bluetooth speaker, a TV browser. It opens a speaker link that carries the speaker key. Guests never get this key, and the room code alone can't fetch audio.

Step 3 of 5

Public address

The address guests and the speaker will open. It goes into the invite link, the QR code and the speaker link. It must be https:// unless it's localhost.

Public URL
Step 4 of 5

Link YouTube Music

rubato streams through your own YouTube Music session, via a cookies.txt file exported from a private window. This file is a login to your Google account: never share it, and delete your copy after uploading.

Playback stays off until YouTube is linked. You can link it later from the host dashboard.
Step 5 of 5

You're set
